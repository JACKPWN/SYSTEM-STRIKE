The IT & Cybersecurity Fighting Game Universe

Learning IT and cybersecurity can be a battle. System Strike turns real-world technology concepts into a fighting RPG where defenders, attackers, systems, and threats become playable characters. Created by Dom Calvello, the universe blends real-world technology with arcade-style combat, creating a visually immersive game world with an educational core.

Defenders, attackers, systems, threats

About

System Strike brings the digital world to life by turning everyday IT and cybersecurity problems into character-driven battles. On one side are the defenders: support teams, network systems, security tools, access controls, and analysts working to keep systems stable. On the other side are the attackers: malware, phishing, botnets, stolen credentials, insider threats, and system failures trying to break through.

Each fighter is built around how their real-world counterpart behaves. Every move, HUD element, warning banner, and attack effect is designed to show players how systems fail, how attackers strike, and how defenders respond.

Health Meter & Energy

System Integrity

Every fighter begins each match with 100% System Integrity, representing their overall ability to stay operational. As fighters take damage, their System Integrity degrades. When it reaches zero, the character enters SYSTEM FAILURE.

Data

Data powers a fighter's strongest abilities. Advanced attacks consume more Data, but deal heavier damage to the opponent's System Integrity.

Connection State

Connection State gives fighters temporary defensive advantages, helping protect their System Integrity from incoming damage.

Together, System Integrity, Data, and Connection State form the core of System Strike’s gameplay. Each fighter uses these resources differently, creating unique mechanics, matchups, and move combinations based on their role in the digital world.

Moves

Every move in System Strike transforms real-world concepts into fast-paced combat. Play as Phishing and unleash attacks such as Typosquatting, Whaling, and Spear Phishing, each brought to life through unique animations, effects, and gameplay mechanics.

Whether you're launching attacks, defending systems, investigating incidents, or restoring services, every move is designed to be both visually exciting and rooted in real technology.
